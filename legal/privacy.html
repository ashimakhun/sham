Privacy Policy

Version 1

Effective date: 20 November 2026

Sham is an app for running courses: meetings, homework, materials, chats and payment records for a teacher, mentors and students. It is operated by Aruzhan Ashimakhun, Kazakhstan ("we"). Contact: ashimakhun.a@gmail.com.

Reminders (a meeting starting, homework due soon, photos about to be deleted) are worked out and scheduled on your phone from data the app already shows you; nothing extra is sent to us for them. Push notifications never contain the name of the person who wrote or did something.

We do not use your data for advertising, we do not sell it, and we do not track you across other apps or websites.

Who processes it for us

  • Supabase stores the database and files and handles sign-in. Servers: Singapore.
  • Expo (Expo's push service) passes push notifications to Apple (iOS) or Google (Android), which deliver them to your phone. They receive your phone's push token and the notification text, which never includes a person's name.
  • Zoom (or another meeting service) is where meetings take place. Sham only stores the meeting link; that service's own policy applies.

Technical connection data

Sham does not store IP addresses in its own data. Our cloud infrastructure may process technical connection metadata, including your IP address, for a short time and only to keep the service secure and to deliver your requests. We do not use it to identify you or link it to your account.

How long we keep it

  • Homework photos are deleted 6 months after the homework is checked. You get warnings 30 and 7 days before, and you can download your photos as a ZIP until then.
  • Your account stays until you delete it.
  • When you delete your account (Profile → Delete account), we delete your profile, username, password, recovery code, phone push registrations, notification settings, your homework and photos, payment records, feedback, likes, Blog posts and PDF files you shared, and uploaded files. Your chat messages and meeting notes stay so that conversations still make sense, but show "Deleted account" instead of your name.

Your rights

You can see and change your display name, language and notification settings in the app, and delete your account at any time. You can also email us, giving your username, to ask what data we hold about you, to correct or block it, to delete it, or to withdraw your consent. We answer within 30 days.

Children

Sham is for people aged 13 and older. Don't create an account if you are under 13, and teachers must not accept one. If we learn that an account belongs to a child under 13, we delete it. Every account joins a workspace only after its teacher accepts it. A teacher who accepts the account of someone under 18 is responsible for having their parent's or guardian's consent where the law requires it. A parent or guardian can ask the teacher or us to delete a child's account at any time.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections. Access rules in the database make sure each person sees only what their role allows, and workspaces never see each other's data.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and tell you in the app.
